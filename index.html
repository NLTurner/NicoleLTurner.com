About Nicole.

I started my career as a teacher, which probably explains some things. I've always been curious about why people do what they do, why organizations work the way they work, and what actually has to change for something to get better.

That curiosity has taken me into organizations, classrooms, businesses, boardrooms, entrepreneurship programs, and onto stages. Today, much of my professional work centers on organizational effectiveness. I diagnose what is producing an unwanted result, connect things that are often examined separately, and help organizations figure out what to do next.

But organizations aren't the only thing I'm curious about. I'm fascinated by how we think, how we change, , getting older, the lives we build, and the beliefs and patterns we carry with us.

Somewhere along the way, all that curiosity turned into businesses, 10 books, a podcast, frameworks, courses, a newsletter, teaching, speaking, mentoring entrepreneurs, judging businesses and big ideas, and serving on nonprofit boards.

I don't fit neatly into one title. I'm okay with that.
